# Hydrodynamic and Aerodynamic Study of WIG Crafts

Welcome to the central repository for my PhD research on Wing-In-Ground (WIG) effect crafts.

## Research Objectives

The primary focus of this research is to advance the understanding and simulation capabilities of WIG crafts operating in dynamic marine environments. The core goals of this project are:

1. **Simulate Interactions:** Model sea deformation and aerodynamic interactions with WIG crafts utilizing mid-fidelity solvers.
2. **Operational Phases:** Simulate the complex dynamics of WIG craft take-off and landing sequences on the water surface.
3. **Maneuverability:** Explore and analyze the maneuverability and stability characteristics of the craft under various operational conditions.

## DUST Aerodynamic Solver

This project utilizes a **modified** version of DUST, a flexible solution for the aerodynamic simulation of complex configurations.

Copyright © 2018-2022 Politecnico di Milano with support from A^3 from Airbus.

DUST comprises a solver, a preprocessor, and a post-processor:

```mermaid
graph LR;
  dust_pre --> dust --> dust_post;

```

- **Preprocessor:** Accepts meshes in CGNS format or in simple point-connectivity definition, or builds the geometry from parametric definitions.
- **Solver:** Takes the geometry generated by the preprocessor, alongside the definition of reference frames and their motion, to calculate the solution and generate results.
- **Post-processor:** Employs the results to elaborate the data according to user requests.
